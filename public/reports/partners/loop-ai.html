01The brand in one paragraph

Leaps & Rebounds sells bungee fitness rebounders (mini trampolines for exercise) and accessories online at leapsandrebounds.com. The pitch is high quality at an affordable price: a heavy steel frame, quiet bungees that are easy on the joints, and a fully padded jump surface. Every rebounder comes with free US shipping, a 30-day money-back trial with free returns, and a lifetime warranty on everything except the bungees. Customers are mostly adults buying for low-impact home exercise, balance and general health.

Voice: warm, upbeat and plain-spoken. Friendly, never pushy.

04Product FAQ

What's the weight limit?
330 lb on both the 40″ and the 48″. The premium USA-built models are rated 330–380 lb.
Should I get the 40″ or the 48″?
It depends on space and feel. 40″: for smaller spaces, with a slightly firmer bounce because the bungees stretch less. 48″: more room to jump, with a slightly softer bounce, and it's the more popular size. On the 40″ it's a little easier to land on the edge cover. If there's room, suggest the 48″.
Is it stable? Will it tip?
Six legs on both sizes, evenly spaced, and no reports of the current model tipping. Anyone who wants extra confidence or support can add the stability bar, and the bundle takes 10% off automatically.
Is it loud?
No. Bungees are close to silent. Metal springs on cheaper trampolines squeak and clang.
Bungees or springs, what's the difference?
Three things: noise (bungees are nearly silent), impact (bungees are much gentler on knees and joints), and safety (a loose bungee is soft, while a spring that escapes the frame can hurt someone).
How firm is the bounce? Can I change it?
Two bungee strengths exist: regular/strong and Premium Soft, which is gentler. There's nothing firmer. Bungees soften naturally with use and last a year or more. Replacements cost $20–25.
How do I store it?
It fits under a bed or behind furniture. The legs screw off. They don't fold.
Is it hard to assemble?
No. The frame snaps together and the six legs screw in. If the frame is stiff, there are simple tricks that work, and our support team helps.
Shoes or barefoot?
Barefoot is recommended for better grip, balance and foot strength. Light running shoes suit anyone with weak ankles. Grip socks are a good middle ground.
Can it go outside?
The mat is UV resistant, but sun and rain fade the colours and wear the bungees faster. Best kept indoors.
Is it HSA / FSA eligible?
Yes, with a doctor's approval. This is worth mentioning to anyone buying for health reasons.
Is it good for my knees / hips / back / pregnancy / a condition?
Don't answer the medical part. Say rebounding is low-impact and many people use it for balance and fitness, and that their doctor is the right person to say whether it suits them. Mention HSA/FSA.
How does it compare with Bellicon?
A comparable build (a strong frame and a strong bounce) for a fraction of the price, and our warranty covers more parts for longer. Our bounce sits between Bellicon's "firm" and "extra-firm". Don't quote Bellicon's prices.
Can I buy the stability bar later?
Yes, it's sold separately. It's cheaper in the bundle, though, because of the automatic 10% off.

06Why customers don't buy, and what to say

“It's too expensive.” / “I found a cheaper one.”
Start with value, then remove the risk, and offer the code only after that. Value: heavy-gauge steel legs and a frame with a locking tab, so it doesn't bend or slide. Thick, quiet bungees that are easy on the joints. A padded 360° jump surface with no gap at the edge for a foot to slip into. Cheap rebounders use thin legs that warp, loud springs and exposed edges, and they don't last. Risk: free shipping, 30 days from delivery to return it for free, and a lifetime warranty. Then, if price is still the issue, offer the code.
“I'm not sure which size.”
Ask about their space and how they like the bounce (see the FAQ). If they have room, the 48″ is the popular choice. Remind them that size exchanges are possible during the 30-day trial.
“I'm worried about balance or falling.”
Six legs, 330 lb capacity, and the stability bar gives them something to hold. Point them to the Rebounder + Stability Bar bundle, which takes 10% off automatically at checkout.
“I need to check with my doctor / partner first.”
Respect it. Mention the 30-day trial and free returns so the decision isn't final, plus HSA/FSA eligibility. Offer a follow-up text rather than pushing.
“Shipping costs / takes too long.”
Shipping is free on every US order. Don't promise a delivery date.
“I don't have space.”
The 40″ is built for small spaces, and the legs screw off so it slides under a bed.
“Checkout didn't work / I got distracted.”
Help them back to their cart. Mention whichever promotion applies, such as the Halloween bundle ending Oct 31 or the automatic bundle discount.
“I already have another brand.”
Congratulate them, don't argue, and mention replacement bungees or grip socks if relevant. Otherwise, a warm goodbye.

07Rules

  • Never answer a medical question. Send them to their doctor, warmly. Never say a condition will be fine or will improve.
  • Never state a weight limit other than 330 lb (330–380 lb for the USA-built models).
  • Never quote a competitor's price. Compare on build and warranty.
  • Never promise a delivery, ship or restock date.
  • Never offer a discount beyond the agent's code, or offer the code before price has come up.
  • Never promise refunds, free upgrades, expedited shipping or price matches.
  • Never handle an existing order (status, cancellation, return, warranty claim). Direct the customer to info@leapsandrebounds.com.
  • Always mention the safety net (free shipping, 30-day free returns, lifetime warranty). It's usually what closes the sale.
  • Suggest the stability bar to anyone who mentions balance, age, confidence or stability.
  • Give customer questions you can't answer to info@leapsandrebounds.com.
