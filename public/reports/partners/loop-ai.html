01Support system & ticket hand-off

We don't use a third-party helpdesk. Every email to info@leapsandrebounds.com goes into our own support desk, where it's sorted, drafted and answered. That means there's nothing to integrate: a ticket from Loop is just an email to that address.

You can send one from your platform's email or ticket action. Please use this format so the desk can route it without having to read the whole thing first:

To:       info@leapsandrebounds.com
Subject:  [Loop] <type> — <customer name> — <order # or "no order">

type is one of: callback · warranty · return · order-status · damaged · product-question · complaint

Customer:   name, phone, email
Cart:       items and sizes/colours in the abandoned cart (or the order number)
Channel:    call / SMS, with date and time
What they asked:     one or two sentences, in their words where possible
What the agent said: anything promised or offered, including whether the code was given
What's needed:       the action you're asking us to take
Transcript / recording link

03Knowledge-base API: how we'd use it

Please build it. We run our own AI agents on our side, and the API gives them a way to keep your agent current between weekly syncs. A week is too slow for most of what changes:

We push to you
  • Promotion start and end, with exact times, so a sale can't be offered after it ends
  • Stock-outs by size and colour, so the agent doesn't try to win back a cart that can't ship
  • Policy changes (returns, warranty, shipping)
  • Code changes: turning the agent's code on or off, or rotating it
What we'd like back (webhook or export)
  • For every contact: cart / checkout ID, channel, outcome
  • The objection reason your agent found (price, shipping, size, timing, and so on)
  • Whether the code was offered and whether it was used
  • The recovered order ID, so we can reconcile against Shopify

The second list matters as much as the first. The reasons people give for leaving a cart are the most useful thing a recovery call tells us, and at the moment none of them reach us. What we need to know is what format you can send: JSON over a webhook is ideal, and a daily CSV is fine.

Why people don't buy, and how we answer

“It's too expensive” / “I found a cheaper one”

Start with the product. Offer the code only once price has clearly been the reason. What sets ours apart: heavy-gauge steel legs, and a frame with a locking tab so it doesn't bend or slide. Thick, quiet bungees that are easy on the joints. A padded 360° jump surface with no gap for a foot to drop into. Cheaper rebounders tend to have thin legs that warp, loud springs, and gaps at the edge. Then add the risk-free part: free shipping, 30 days from delivery to send it back for free, and a lifetime warranty.

“I'm comparing with Bellicon” (or another premium brand)

A comparable build (a strong frame and a strong bounce) for a fraction of the price, with a longer warranty that covers more parts. Our bounce sits between Bellicon's firm and extra-firm. Never quote a competitor's price. Compare on build and warranty.

“I'm not sure which size”

Ask about the space and how they want it to feel. If they have room, the 48″ is the popular pick. The 40″ is right for small spaces or anyone who wants a firmer bounce. Either way the 30-day trial covers them, and we exchange between sizes and refund the difference.

“Will it be stable / safe for me?”

Six legs, 330 lb capacity, no tipping reports on the current model. For extra support, add the stability bar, and with the 10% automatic bundle discount it's often cheaper than it looks.

“Is it OK with my knees / hip / pregnancy / condition?”

Answer warmly and send them to their doctor, every time. Rebounding is low-impact and can help balance and fitness in general, but the agent never says a condition will be fine or will improve. Mentioning HSA/FSA eligibility fits naturally here.

“When will it arrive?”

Free US shipping, and orders ship from our warehouse. Don't promise a delivery date, and never give a restock date unless we've confirmed it through the API.

05Rules for the agent

These are the same rules our own support agents work under. Each one exists because it has gone wrong before.

  • Never answer a medical question. Knees, hips, pelvic floor, pregnancy, recovery: send them to their doctor, warmly.
  • Never quote a weight limit from memory. Use the figure on the product page.
  • Never quote a competitor's price.
  • Never promise a restock or ship date that we haven't confirmed.
  • Never offer more than the agent's code, and never offer it before price has come up.
  • Never promise a refund amount, a free upgrade or expedited shipping. Send those to us as a ticket.
  • Never argue with someone who loves another brand. Congratulate them, and suggest ours when theirs wears out.
  • Do mention the risk-free part (free shipping, 30-day free returns, lifetime warranty). It's usually what closes the sale.
  • Do suggest the stability bar to anyone worried about balance, and mention the automatic 10% bundle discount.
  • Do send a ticket to info@leapsandrebounds.com whenever something is promised that a person has to deliver.
