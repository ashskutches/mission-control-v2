Sales are not falling day over day: the last five weeks beat the same weeks last year by 21%. The damage is older. 2026 is 26% behind 2025, almost all of it lost in the first half of the year, and it is a traffic problem rather than a conversion problem.

Direct traffic: influencers and the welcome offer

"Direct" is people who arrive with no tracked source: typed URLs, influencer links, podcast mentions, untagged ads. It lost 639 purchases (−26%). Discount codes show where those buyers went.

  • The three biggest 2025 influencer codes lost 273 orders, about $63k. New partners (OFFGRID 97, ANGIEVIP 62, MARC10 23, ADRI 22) won back part of it.
  • Fewer new visitors are taking the welcome offer. HELLO10 is down 212 orders, which follows the drop in new visitors rather than a problem with the pop-up.
  • Sale events work. Memorial Day grew from 185 to 251 orders, and new events (Valentine's 196, 10 Years 110) added about $63k.
  • Session counts are inflated by junk. From Oct 2025 to Mar 2026 direct sessions ran 34k–62k a month converting at 0.3–0.6%, which looks like bot traffic. Purchases are the reliable measure.

Facebook and Instagram

Social is the steepest decline: purchases from social referrals fell 54% (619 → 287). Facebook went from 336 to 137 purchases, Instagram from 139 to 62, YouTube from 118 to 75. Facebook sessions halved over the last 90 days, and Pinterest nearly vanished (1,805 → 248 sessions).

  • Meta claims far more than Shopify sees. In September Meta reported 79 purchases from $3.9k of spend; Shopify credited 5 to Facebook. Untagged ad clicks land as "direct", so part of the direct decline may be a paid-social decline.
  • The open question is spend. Meta spend is only recorded from June 2026 ($0.3k–$3.9k a month, on and off). If 2025 spend was materially higher, a spend cut is the most likely single cause of both the social and direct losses.

Google and AI search

AI is taking some clicks, but it is a smaller loss than direct traffic or Facebook. Google-referred purchases fell 11% (1,654 → 1,473, Jan–Sep), and Google clicks fell 18% (19,944 → 16,340, Jul–Sep).

  • The branded drop matters most. A quarter fewer clicks on our own name, at the same rank, means something above us is answering or selling.

  • The early-2026 traffic spike was empty. Impressions jumped from about 0.4M to 1.7M a month on off-topic blog posts; clicks rose but Google purchases fell.
  • AI chatbots send almost nothing yet. ChatGPT sent 69 sessions and 2 purchases in 90 days; all AI assistants together produced about 15 purchases in 2026.

The blog → advertorial → product funnel

Status, Oct 13: the 15% code behind every blog button had been deleted after Sep 13, so readers were promised a discount they did not get at checkout. A replacement code, 15OFFBLOG, is created and the theme change is staged for publishing.
  • Blog visitors almost never buy, before or after the change. About 2,300–2,600 sessions a week land on a blog post; they produced 7 purchases in five weeks of July–August and 4 in the five weeks to Oct 11.
  • More readers now add to cart, but don't finish (23 cart adds in four July weeks, 35 from Sep 14–Oct 11). A missing discount at checkout fits that pattern.
  • The advertorial adds a step. It has no cart, so it is one more page between a reader and the buy button.
  • The funnel can be judged once the code works. Run it four weeks, then test blog → product page directly against blog → advertorial.
