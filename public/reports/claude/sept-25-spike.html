What happened

+$1,760

A first-time customer bought eight rebounders in one order

Order #48845, $1,584 after the 10OFFTRAMP code. They first landed on the rebounder product page three days earlier and came back directly to buy. This single order is about 50% of the gap between yesterday and a typical day. At this size it is most likely a studio, gym or group buyer.

+$525

The Furry Mat Cover suddenly started selling

Seven orders yesterday. It had one in the previous 12 days. Sessions landing on its product page went from 6, 12 and 6 on Sep 22–24 to 115 on Sep 25, and 12 of those added to cart.

Across Sep 25–26, most of those visits arrived direct on a phone (109 of 182), with Google (43) and YouTube (14) behind. They were spread from mid-morning to evening, peaking around noon and 6–7pm Pacific. That is what a shared link looks like: a video, a group post, a text. Our data cannot say whose. The product did not change: same $75 price, same $100 compare-at, listed since January 2025.

+$489

The Barbara O'Neill Collective store sent a burst of orders

Five Shopify Collective orders for $655, against an average of about $166 a day over the prior week. Four of them came within two hours of each other in the afternoon. The demand came from their side, and we have no view into what they did.

+$629

Everyday demand was up too, and it started on Thursday

Search sessions rose to 639, against an average of 413 on Sep 20–24. Search visits that added to cart went from about 21 to 50, and search checkouts from about 4 to 9. The Google Ads campaign 23924847266 was the first touch on 8 of the web orders. Add-to-cart had already jumped on Sep 24 (65 sessions), so the lift began a day before the spike.

Automated emails closed 3 orders: two abandoned-cart emails and one welcome-series email.

What it was not

An email campaign

None went out on Sep 25. Store Credit sent on Sep 23 and Lifetime Warranty on Sep 26.

A traffic spike

Sessions were up 18%. Add-to-cart was up 170%. The people who came were readier to buy.

A price change

The rebounder and the mat cover were at their usual prices, and no new discount code was involved.

What to do next

  1. Find out who shared the mat cover. If anyone knows of a video or post from Sep 25, that is a partner worth contacting again. Someone edited the product listing on Sep 26 at about 10:30am Pacific, which may be related.
  2. Check #48845 before it ships. Make sure the billing and shipping details look right for a first-time order of this size. If it is a studio, it is a wholesale lead.
  3. Ask the Collective partner what they ran. A partner channel that can send five orders in an afternoon is worth understanding.
  4. Watch whether the lift holds. If add-to-cart stays near 60+ through next week, the Sep 24 change is real and worth tracing to its source.
